The five

ARed. iOS draws the strip and is handed a theme-colour. This is what the app does now. BGreen. iOS draws the strip and is handed nothing, so the page background is the only candidate. CPurple. The page runs under the clock and paints the band itself. Total control — but check the clock is still readable. DSolid black. The third Apple setting, for completeness. EOrange. No Apple settings at all — how this app was built for its whole life until today.

What each answer means

  1. A goes red — the live fix is correct and something else (a stale install, or a cached page) is why you're not seeing it. Cheap to fix.
  2. A doesn't, but B goes green — iOS is taking the strip from the page background. I make the page background the chrome colour and it's done, no metas involved.
  3. Only C goes purple — iOS won't hand us the strip, so the page takes it over and paints it. Guaranteed to work; the only question is whether your clock stays readable on a light strip.
  4. Nothing changes on any of them — then it isn't the iOS status bar at all and I've been fixing the wrong thing all day. Tell me and I'll ask you to describe exactly where the strip sits.

One trip through this replaces about ten more rounds of me deploying a guess and asking you to look.
